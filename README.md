🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer shopping behavior using **Python, PostgreSQL, and Microsoft Power BI**.

The analysis is based on 3,900 customer purchase records. The objective is to identify purchasing patterns, customer segments, product preferences, discount behavior, and subscription trends to support data-driven business decisions.

🎯 Business Problem

A retail company wants to understand customer shopping behavior to improve sales, customer satisfaction, customer engagement, and long-term loyalty.

This project explores customer demographics, product categories, discounts, reviews, shipping preferences, and purchasing patterns to identify actionable business insights.

📊 Dataset Information

**Total Records:** 3,900
**Total Columns:** 18
**Missing Values:** 37
- **Missing Value Column:** Review Rating

The dataset includes customer demographics, purchase information, product categories, subscription status, shipping type, discount information, payment methods, and purchase frequency.

## 🔄 Project Workflow

### 1️⃣ Python – Data Preparation and EDA

- Loaded the dataset using Pandas.
- Explored dataset structure using `info()` and `describe()`.
- Checked missing values and data quality.
- Imputed missing Review Rating values using the median rating of each product category.
- Standardized column names using snake_case.
- Created an `age_group` column using age quantiles.
- Created `purchase_frequency_days` using purchase frequency mapping.
- Checked redundancy between discount and promo code columns.
- Removed the redundant `promo_code_used` column.
- Connected Python to PostgreSQL and loaded the cleaned dataset.

### 2️⃣ PostgreSQL – SQL Analysis

Performed SQL analysis to answer 10 business questions:

1. 💰 Revenue generated by male and female customers.
2. 🛍️ Customers using discounts while spending above the average purchase amount.
3. ⭐ Top 5 products based on average review ratings.
4. 🚚 Average purchase amount comparison between Standard and Express shipping.
5. 👥 Spending and revenue comparison between subscribers and non-subscribers.
6. 🏷️ Top 5 products with the highest discount percentage.
7. 📊 Customer segmentation into New, Returning, and Loyal.
8. 🏆 Top 3 most purchased products within each category.
9. 🔁 Relationship between repeat buyers and subscription status.
10. 📈 Revenue contribution by age group.

## 💡 Key Findings

- Approximately 73% of customers are non-subscribers and 27% are subscribers.
- Express shipping customers have a higher average purchase amount than Standard shipping customers in the SQL analysis.
- Customer purchasing behavior varies across product categories and age groups.
- The analysis identifies high-spending discount users and discount-dependent products.
- Customer segmentation helps distinguish New, Returning, and Loyal shoppers.

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

- **📈 Boost Subscriptions:** Promote exclusive benefits to encourage subscription adoption.
- **🤝 Customer Loyalty Programs:** Reward repeat buyers to improve retention.
- **🏷️ Review Discount Policy:** Balance discount-driven sales with profitability.
- **⭐ Product Positioning:** Highlight top-rated and best-selling products.
- **🎯 Targeted Marketing:** Focus on high-revenue customer segments and express-shipping users.
